## Launch measurement plan

Begin with an organic post to the named owner-controlled channel. Use one reviewed screenshot/demo, no fabricated reviews or urgency. Review visits, completed first trips, checkout starts, successful paid access and support issues before paid promotion. Do not start paid ads without a named account and approved spend.

Not supported claims: real-time booking, live flight/hotel prices, external AI concierge, collaborative group planning, automatic split-cost payments or push/email travel reminders.
